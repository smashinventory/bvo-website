  • For: one clear action. The capture form was built precisely because these pages were dead ends, and nothing competes with it.
  • Against: a shopper ready to spend $2,400 today is told to wait for an email, when you may well have a near-identical vanity in stock.

My read: Option A, but with the two asks ranked rather than stacked — the capture form stays the visually dominant one, and the call sits beneath it as the quieter alternative, exactly as drawn. On a product this expensive, a person who wants to talk is worth more than a list signup, and an expert can move them to something that is in stock.

What this costs if you pick A: nothing extra to build — it is one conditional in the template either way. The only real cost is a busier out-of-stock page.
