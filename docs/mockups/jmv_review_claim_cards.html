One open question

Breckenridge

Breckenridge is your #2 seller and the one collection where the two retailers flatly disagree — 3.4 at Home Depot across 11 reviews, 4.6 at Ferguson across 21. Combined that is 4.2, which is arithmetically true and, I think, misleading in both directions.

On a card grid its blank is more conspicuous than it would be on a landing page, because Brittany's 4.5 sits two cards away. The footnote's second sentence is what makes that survivable — it says "too few independent reviews," not "poorly rated."

My recommendation stands: leave it off. If you would rather show something, the honest version is the Home Depot figure alone with its count, and that means publishing a 3.4 next to your second-best seller. Your call — I will build whichever you pick, and the gate will enforce it either way.
